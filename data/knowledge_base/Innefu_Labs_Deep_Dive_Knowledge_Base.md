### 11. AI Vision — Image and Video Analytics
The product catalogue positions AI Vision as an AI-powered security surveillance system for image and video analytics.

- Listed features include face recognition, object tracking, crowd analysis, privacy masking and system health monitoring.
- Face recognition compares facial representations. Performance can vary with image quality, camera angle, lighting, occlusion and demographic factors.

- Crowd analysis may estimate crowd patterns or movement, but exact supported metrics are not detailed in the catalogue.

### 12. Innsight — OSINT
The catalogue describes Innsight as an OSINT/network intelligence platform for monitoring global threats.

- Listed features include 360-degree profiling, network analysis, threat modelling, sentiment analysis and event prediction.
- OSINT means intelligence derived from publicly available sources collected and analysed under applicable law and platform rules.
- Network analysis can help explore relationships among public entities, accounts, topics or events. Sentiment analysis and threat modelling are context-dependent and may be noisy.
- Public availability does not automatically make collection, retention or profiling lawful or appropriate. Minimise data and document purpose.
- Do not claim access to private accounts, restricted systems or covert data sources based solely on the OSINT label.

### 13. InteleLinx — CDR/IPDR
The catalogue positions InteleLinx as a telecom data analytics platform for investigative intelligence.

- Listed capabilities include multiple CDR and IPDR analysis, IMEI analysis, relationship/link analysis, group analysis, Google Earth integration and tower CDR/IPDR analysis.
- CDR commonly means Call Detail Record; IPDR commonly means Internet Protocol Detail Record. Such records may contain communications or network-session metadata, depending on provider and format.
- IMEI is an equipment identifier associated with mobile devices. Handling this data requires lawful authority and strict controls.
- A relationship or co-occurrence is not proof of wrongdoing. Location precision depends on source data and network architecture.
- Never provide instructions to obtain telecom records unlawfully. Emphasise lawful access, minimisation, retention and auditability.
